---
title: "CDC-002: Insufficient synchronizer depth"
linkTitle: CDC-002
weight: 101
---

**Severity:** warning

A synchronizer chain exists but is shorter than the site minimum
(--sync-depth, default 2): raise the depth or lower the bar.
Silent at the default unless a project explicitly requires more.

## Suppression marks

This rule honors: `(* cdc_static *)`, `(* cdc_sync *)`.

## Passing examples

- `good_default_silent.sv` — clean (default silent)
